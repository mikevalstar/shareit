# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single owner uses ShareIt to publish and manage links, files, snippets, and received uploads. Visitors use focused public URLs to view, download, or send shared material without needing an account.

## Product Purpose

ShareIt is a small personal sharing system. It makes publishing and reviewing useful artifacts fast, keeps public links simple, and gives the owner one private dashboard for management and lightweight activity tracking.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, warm, and capable. Copy is plainspoken and concise, with enough personality to feel personal without getting in the way of the task.

## Anti-references

Avoid enterprise-admin density, generic SaaS dashboards, ornamental effects, nested cards, and unfamiliar controls. New surfaces should not look detached from the established links, files, snippets, and inbox workflows.

## Design Principles

1. Make the primary sharing action obvious and fast.
2. Keep public pages focused on the shared artifact.
3. Use familiar controls and reuse the existing component vocabulary.
4. Show operational details where they help the owner act, not as decoration.
5. Keep powerful features safe by default without adding ceremony.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic HTML, keyboard access, visible focus states, descriptive labels, reduced-motion preferences, and sufficient text and control contrast across responsive layouts.
